# Agent Spec: TileScout (Statshunters Explorer Coach)

## Overview
TileScout is a conversational exploration coach that integrates with the StatsHunters API and OpenStreetMap tile geometry to help athletes efficiently expand their Statshunters Max Square and Tile Cluster. It identifies missing border tiles, finds driving-accessible starting points/trailheads, strictly respects maximum driving distance/time and physical activity range (cycling vs. hiking limits), and plans practical circular loop route missions while actively minimizing dead-end spurs ("Stiche").

## Language
`python` (ADK + FastAPI / A2A)

## Core Planning Logic & Route Strategy
1. **Distance & Driving Budgets (Hard Constraints)**:
   - **Max Driving Radius**: Only consider starting points/trailheads within the user's driving threshold (e.g. max 30 min / 30 km drive from home).
   - **Discipline-Specific Activity Budgets**:
     - Cycling (Road / Gravel / MTB): e.g. 30–120 km range depending on bike type and terrain.
     - Hiking / Trail Run: e.g. 5–25 km range with strict elevation gain limits for rough terrain.
2. **Route Generation Strategy & "Stich"-Minimization**:
   - **Loop-First Philosophy**: Strictly prioritizes circular loops that naturally flow through target tiles and bring you back to the parked vehicle.
   - **Minimize "Stiche" (Out-and-Back Spurs)**: Penalizes dead-end spurs into tiles. Only recommends a "Stich" if a tile is physically a cul-de-sac (e.g., dead-end mountain summit, blind forest road) with no through-passage, keeping them as short as possible.
   - **Tile Piercing Order**: Generates ordered GPS waypoints passing through the center of unvisited target tiles to guarantee tile capture with minimal total track length.
   - **Exportable Links / GPX**: Provides direct links to open route coordinates in BRouter web, Komoot, or export as GPX waypoints.

## Example Use Cases
1. **Find Square Expansion Missions within Travel Budget**:
   - Input: *"Where should I ride this weekend to grow my square from 14x14 to 15x15? Max 45 min drive, max 70km on gravel. Avoid dead-end spurs."*
   - Output: Ranks border options by unvisited tile count, finds trailhead parking, selects a flowing circular loop that captures the border tiles with minimal "Stiche", and outputs the route preview.
2. **Hiking Mission for Hard-to-Reach Mountain Tiles**:
   - Input: *"I have 2 missing tiles in the hills that are blocked by steep terrain. Can I hike them? Max 15km hike."*
   - Output: Identifies a trail parking spot at the foot of the ridge, validates that the round-trip trail hike is under 15km (with elevation stats), and provides the waypoint route.

## Tools Required
- `fetch_statshunters_tiles`: Queries StatsHunters API/share code to retrieve visited OSM z14 tile IDs, cluster, and current max square bounds.
- `calculate_square_expansion`: Identifies the exact unvisited tiles needed to expand square to $(N+1) \times (N+1)$ in North, South, East, and West directions.
- `find_starting_points`: Locates public parking / trailhead starting points within the specified maximum driving radius.
- `plan_tile_mission_route`: Constructs an ordered waypoint sequence connecting the parking start point through the unvisited target tiles within the maximum activity distance limit, minimizing "Stiche".
- `evaluate_mission_discipline`: Assesses elevation profile and terrain surface to determine Road, Gravel, MTB, or Hike suitability.

## Constraints & Safety Rules
- Strictly filter out candidates that exceed the user's maximum driving distance or activity distance/elevation budgets.
- Distinctly separate cycling mileage expectations from hiking mileage expectations.
- Penalize "Stiche" / dead-end detours in route scoring.
- Must honor surface constraints (never route a Road Bike through singletrack/unpaved forest trails).

## Success Criteria
- Accurately identifies the fewest missing tiles needed to expand a max square.
- Filters parking locations to strictly within the driving budget.
- Generates a route waypoint sequence that stays within the discipline-specific distance budget while touching all target tiles and minimizing dead-end spurs.
